## Verdict

Two claims, two verdicts:

1. "The mathematics of the rule is novel": OVERREACHING. The rule is water-filling with a per-agent
   level proportional to latent type, a two-line KKT corollary of the harmonic production function.
   Standing relatives: information-theoretic water-filling (common level, Cover-Thomas); the
   poverty-gap top-up g = (z - y)+ in targeted transfers (common level, type-independent);
   marginal-equalization budget allocation in marketing/OR. The paper must NOT claim mathematical
   novelty; it should cite water-filling as the standing precedent, which converts the risk into
   positioning (the precedent legitimates the derivation, cf. type-B methodological grounding).

2. "The rule qua statement about science funding is novel": SURVIVES at this strength: no prior
   statement of a fund-the-talent-resource-gap rule found in the science-funding literature.
   Checked (2026-08-05): the closest recent paper, Sorzano & Pueche-Granados, "Research Funding as a
   Decision Problem Under Heavy-Tailed Uncertainty" (arXiv 2604.22793, Apr 2026): empirical
   bibliometric signal + biased-lottery mechanism, no two-input model, no closed-form rule, no
   top-up allocation. Also surveyed: concentration/dispersal literature (Mongeon et al.; Aagaard
   et al. QSS 2020), lottery literature (Avin's simulation; SNSF Bayesian ranking + lottery,
   arXiv 2102.09958), peer-review predictivity (Fang et al.). None state the rule.
