## Discovery during Package C: the review signal is observed once, not redrawn each round
- The code (model.R, run_simulation_T) draws the review signal sigma_k once per run, before the strategy loop, and every round's posterior uses that same score. Both documents said "drawn afresh in each round"; that description was false. Corrected everywhere (Model / Methods / SI Methods): the review signal is K_i + xi_i, observed once at the start; a reviewer's misjudgment persists. The referee's concern that fresh independent reviews inflate Fig. 2B does not apply; the variant with a fresh review every round is the new robustness check (Package C3), reported in Appendix E / SI Text 5.
