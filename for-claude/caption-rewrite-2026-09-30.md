== fig1-frontier.tex
OLD \caption{The gap rule in one population at two budgets. Each point is a researcher, placed by resources and capability. The two lines are the funding frontiers $R = cK$ at a smaller budget $b_1$ and a larger budget $b_2$; a larger budget raises $c$ and flattens the frontier. Researchers to the left of a frontier are funded at that budget: black points at both budgets, gray points only at the larger budget, open points at neither. Each arrow is a grant at the smaller budget, which moves its researcher exactly to that budget's frontier; at the larger budget every black and gray researcher receives a grant that moves it to the outer frontier (arrows not drawn).}
NEW \caption{The gap rule in one population at two budgets. Each point is a researcher, placed by resources $R$ and capability $K$. Each line is a funding frontier $R = cK$: the steeper line for the smaller budget $b_1$, the flatter line for the larger budget $b_2$. Researchers to the left of a frontier are funded at that budget: black points at both budgets, gray points only at the larger, open points at neither. Each arrow is a grant at the smaller budget and moves its researcher onto that frontier; at the larger budget every black and gray researcher is moved onto the outer frontier (arrows omitted).}
