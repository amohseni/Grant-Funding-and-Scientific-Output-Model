When does looking ahead beat funding the leaders?

A forward-looking Bayesian grant planner beats a myopic one across the board — and by more as knowledge compounds faster and the funding horizon lengthens. Its edge comes from front-loading grants to grow future capacity; the value of the peer-review signal, meanwhile, is set almost entirely by inequality × precision.

Figure 1 · horizon_growth · the headline

Foresight always helps — and more as knowledge compounds

Forward minus myopic expected output (S8 − S5), across horizon T and knowledge-growth rate ε. Deeper teal = larger forward advantage. The gain is positive in every cell, grows steadily with growth ε, and — once growth is fast enough — keeps climbing with the horizon.

The mechanism is front-loading. Forward's schedule center-of-mass b_idx rises with growth — 0.51 at ε=0.05 to 0.62 at ε=0.85, and higher still with the horizon — as it front-loads grants to compound knowledge. The faster knowledge compounds, the more that early investment pays, which is exactly why the forward advantage tracks ε. Even at ε→0, where front-loading buys nothing, forward keeps a small edge from the information its early grants reveal.

Model & method

  • Production λ = γ·KR/(K+R), compounding knowledge K ← K + ε·KR/(K+R), non-persistent grants, Poisson observations. 9 strategies (no-fund → myopic → forward CE-planner).
  • Forward planner generalized from the 2-round v5 to T rounds (receding-horizon certainty-equivalent with one-step information anticipation). T=2 reproduces v5 bit-identically (max Δ ≈ 4×10⁻¹⁴), and forward's advantage is now stable as the greedy granularity is refined.
  • Manifest: 13 sweeps, 188 cells, 200 trials/cell, n=50 researchers, M=400 posterior samples; common random numbers across cells. Mean-E[R] budget, default b=0.5.
  • All §6 validity assertions pass. CE-vs-scenario valuation gap at T=2 = −0.26% (near-exact). At ε→0 the schedule is uniform yet forward retains a small edge — pure information value, distinct from the compounding channel that scales with ε.

Method note: an earlier build showed a spurious high-T "decline" that traced to a stochastic resampling step in the planner's belief update — it corrupted the finite-difference marginals, mildly at 2 rounds but severely as the horizon and greedy resolution grew. With a deterministic reweight (and M raised to 400) the forward advantage is positive and stable across granularity; these figures reflect the corrected planner. Signal-value cells at the heaviest tail (α_K→1) see importance-sampling ESS/M < 0.1 for ~¼ of researchers; read with mild caution.
